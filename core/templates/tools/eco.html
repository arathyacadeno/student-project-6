Travel lighter

  • Take KSRTC buses or trains between towns instead of private cars.
  • Choose small homestays that hire locally.
  • Carry a reusable bottle. Say no to single-use plastic.
  • Keep to marked trails and never feed wildlife.
  • Buy spices and crafts from local cooperatives.
